Statistical Optimization

At a glance

Problem

Modern robust statistical targets often lead to difficult minimax, nonconvex, or large-scale optimization problems.

Approach

Algorithms exploit the structure of distributional robustness, invariance, and heterogeneous data.

Goal

Make complex targets computationally practical while preserving interpretable statistical guarantees.
