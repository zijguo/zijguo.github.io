Applications to Genetics and Health Data

At a glance

Problem

Genetic and clinical data are heterogeneous, instruments can be imperfect, and privacy constraints can prevent patient-level sharing.

Approach

Robust causal, federated, and transfer-learning methods combine evidence without assuming that every instrument or data source is equally reliable.

Scientific aim

Support reproducible biomarker discovery and durable risk prediction across biobanks, hospitals, and patient populations.
