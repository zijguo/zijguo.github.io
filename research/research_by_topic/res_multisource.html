Multi-source Learning

At a glance

Problem

Studies, hospitals, and environments often disagree, so simple pooling can be unstable or misleading.

Approach

Minimax and distributionally robust targets guard against adverse mixtures and shifts across sources.

Inference and computation

Efficient algorithms and valid uncertainty quantification make optimization-defined targets statistically actionable.
