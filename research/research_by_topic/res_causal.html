Reliable Causal Inference

At a glance

Problem

Unmeasured confounding and invalid instruments can make causal conclusions fragile or unidentified.

Approach

Identification and inference methods remain meaningful when some instruments may violate the usual validity assumptions.

Current directions

Nonregular uncertainty quantification and causal relationships that remain stable across environments.
