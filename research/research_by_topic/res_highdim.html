High-Dimensional Uncertainty Quantification

At a glance

Problem

When variables outnumber observations, ordinary regression output can look precise while remaining unstable.

Approach

Confidence intervals, tests, and multiple-testing procedures designed for high-dimensional linear and generalized linear models.

Applications

Hidden confounding, endogeneity, genetic relatedness, mediation analysis, and semi-supervised risk prediction.
