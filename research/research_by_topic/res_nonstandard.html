Nonstandard Inference

At a glance

Problem

Standard Wald intervals can fail for optimization-defined, data-adaptive, or nonregular targets.

Approach

Perturbation, resampling, and stability-based constructions accommodate nonsmooth limits and flexible nuisance learning.

Goal

Confidence statements with reliable coverage when classical normal approximations are too optimistic.
